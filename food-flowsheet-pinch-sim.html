Second-law breakdown

Exin = Σ stream exergy in + utility exergy delivered + electrical work; ExD = Exin − Σ stream exergy out − exergy rejected to cold utilities. ηII = (Σ exergy of non-waste outlets) / Exin. Ambient-air intakes carry slightly negative exergy because the dead state is liquid water and dry air; this does not change any unit's destruction.
